# SmartBudget Manager

Author: MADENE Meriem Rahma | 2nd year CPI, Group 2 | ESI-SBA 2026/2027

SmartBudget Manager is an Excel file that helps you follow your monthly income and expenses. It calculates your balance and warns you when you spend more than your budget.

## What it does
- Records your income and expenses
- Puts each expense in a category (Food, Transport, Rent, Health, Education)
- Calculates total income, total expenses and the monthly balance
- Shows a chart of expenses by category
- Shows **OVER BUDGET** in red when you spend more than your budget

## What you need
- Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer
- The file `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`

## How to start
1. Open `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`. If you see a yellow bar, click **Enable Editing**.
2. Go to the **Categories** sheet and set a monthly budget for each category.
3. Go to the **Transactions** sheet. In the first empty row, enter the date and description, choose the **Type** and **Category** from the lists, and type the amount in **Income** or **Expense**.
4. Go to the **Dashboard** sheet to see your totals, balance, chart and budget status.
5. Press **Ctrl + S** to save.

## The three sheets
| Sheet | Use |
|---|---|
| Transactions | Enter income and expenses |
| Categories | Set the budget of each category |
| Dashboard | See the results (do not type here) |

## Good to know
- Use rows 4 to 50 only.
- Choose the category from the list. Do not type it.
- Do not type in the **Month** column or on the **Dashboard**.
- The Dashboard adds all transactions. For a new month, make a copy and delete the old transactions.
